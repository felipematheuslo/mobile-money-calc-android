# 🇰🇪 Safaricom M-Pesa Kenya — Official Tariffs and Business Rules Guide

Official reference guide detailing all transaction rules, regulatory thresholds, and tariff structures for Safaricom M-Pesa in Kenya.

* **Official Source:** [Safaricom Consumer Tariffs & Limits](https://www.safaricom.co.ke/main-mpesa/m-pesa-for-you/tariffs-limits/consumer-tariffs-limits)

---

## 1. M-Pesa Account Limits & Rules

| Parameter | Official Limit (KES) | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Maximum Account Balance** | `500,000 KES` | Regulatory limit set by the Central Bank of Kenya (CBK) |
| **Maximum Daily Transaction Limit** | `500,000 KES` | Maximum accumulated transactions across 24 hours |
| **Maximum Amount Per Single Transaction** | `250,000 KES` | Single-transfer transaction cap |
| **Minimum Cash Withdrawal at Agent** | `50 KES` | M-Pesa physical agent outlets do not process withdrawals below 50 KES |
| **Minimum Transfer Amount** | `1 KES` | Minimum allowable wallet-to-wallet transfer |

### Operational Notes & Support
* **M-Pesa Self-Reversal:** If funds are sent to the wrong number, the sender can forward the transaction confirmation SMS to **`456`** to initiate an instant transaction freeze and self-reversal process.
* **Cash Deposits:** Can only be made directly into the customer's own registered M-Pesa account upon presenting a valid Kenyan National ID, Passport, Military ID, or Foreigner Certificate.

## 5. Lipa Na M-Pesa (Buy Goods / Till Number Payments)

Purchases at formal retail outlets, supermarkets, restaurants, and pharmacy tills:

### A. For the Customer / Consumer:
* **Customer Charge:** **`0 KES (100% FREE)`**
* *Exception:* Authorized fuel stations where the merchant may pass transaction costs to the paying customer.

### B. For the Merchant (Receiving Payments):
* **0 – 200 KES:** Free (0%)
* **201 – 500 KES:** Free (0%)
* **501 – 36,363 KES:** **0.55%** of transaction amount
* **Above 36,363 KES:** Flat fee of **200 KES**

## 6. Free Services (Zero Tariff)

The following core actions carry no charge across the M-Pesa ecosystem:
* All cash deposits (*All Deposits*)
* M-Pesa account registration (*Registration*)
* Purchasing mobile airtime through M-Pesa (*Airtime purchase*)
* Account balance enquiry (*Balance Enquiry*)
* PIN changes (*Change PIN*)
