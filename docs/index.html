Mobile Money Calculator

Privacy Policy

Application: MomoCalc: Mobile Money Fees
Effective Date: August 24, 2026

1. Overview & Core Commitment

MomoCalc: Mobile Money Fees ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed as a utility tool to provide transparent, instant calculations for Mobile Money transaction tariffs and agent cash-out fees (such as Safaricom M-Pesa, MTN Mobile Money, and Airtel Money).

🛡️ 100% Offline by Design: MomoCalc does not require an account, does not collect personal identifiers, and performs all calculations locally on your device.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Because privacy is our priority:

  • No Financial Data: We never see, store, or transmit amounts you enter, account numbers, wallet balances, or PINs.
  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or location.
  • No Account Required: You can use all core calculation features immediately without any registration or sign-in.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Services

To keep the App free for everyone, MomoCalc displays advertisements using Google AdMob (Google Play Services). Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personal device information to serve ads:

  • Device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID).
  • Device model, operating system version, and general network connection type.
  • Ad performance and diagnostic metrics (such as impressions and click statistics).

For more details on how Google processes and protects advertising data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

4. Device Permissions & Local Storage

The App requests only minimal standard Android system permissions:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required exclusively to load advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • Local App Preferences (SharedPreferences): Used strictly on your local device to remember your last selected operator and country (e.g., Safaricom Kenya or MTN Uganda). This preference never leaves your phone.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Our service is not directed to anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or maintain personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take necessary measures.

6. Data Security & International Users (GDPR / CCPA)

Since no user personal data or transaction history is stored on our servers, there is no risk of your calculation data being leaked, sold, or shared. For ad personalization preferences, users can reset their Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time via Android System Settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements or app enhancements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
