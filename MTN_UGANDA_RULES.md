# 🇺🇬 MTN Mobile Money Uganda — Official Tariffs and Business Rules Guide

Official reference guide detailing all transaction rules, regulatory thresholds, and tariff structures for MTN MoMo in Uganda.

* **Official Source:** [MTN Uganda Mobile Money Tariffs](https://www.mtn.co.ug/tariffs/mobile-money-tariffs/)

---

## 1. MTN MoMo Account Limits & Rules

| Parameter | Official Limit (UGX) | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Maximum Account Balance** | `20,000,000 UGX` | Maximum balance allowed in an MTN MoMo wallet |
| **Maximum Transaction Limit** | `5,000,000 UGX` | Single-transaction cap for transfers and withdrawals |
| **Minimum Transaction Amount** | `500 UGX` | Minimum allowable transaction amount |
| **Minimum Account Balance** | `0 UGX` | No mandatory minimum balance required |

### Statutory Government Tax (Uganda)
* **Excise Tax on Cash Withdrawals:** The Government of Uganda levies a statutory **0.5% tax** on the cash withdrawal amount. 
* **Automatic Deduction:** Taxes are automatically deducted from the user's MoMo account alongside the agent fee during withdrawal transactions.

## 4. Free Services (Zero Tariff)

The following services have no service fee on MTN Uganda:
* Access to MoMo account while roaming (*FREE*)
* MoMo App statement access (*FREE*)
* Mini Statement access via USSD `*165*8#` (*FREE*)
* MTN MoMo Registration (*FREE*)
* Depositing cash onto MoMo account at agents (*FREE*)
* Buying airtime for self or another number (*FREE*)
* Purchasing voice, internet, and SMS bundles (*FREE*)
* Changing MoMo PIN (*FREE*)
* Checking Account Balance (*FREE*)
